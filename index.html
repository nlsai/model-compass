Before you build

Prices are OpenRouter’s listed USD input and output rates per million text tokens. Provider routing, caching, images, reasoning, additional usage, and credit fees may affect the final bill. Check the source before use. Free hosted access can be rate-limited, removed, or subject to provider data policies. OpenRouter documents 50 free-model requests/day for accounts with less than $10 in purchased credits, and 1,000/day once at least $10 has been purchased; check current limits. Local inference avoids a hosted API fee but needs hardware, electricity and setup. Never assume a model can directly access Jira or an API: your application must supply tools, credentials, permissions and review gates.

Context values are provider-advertised maximums, not a guarantee of effective recall or available memory. Quality and practicality scores are editorial estimates to guide experiments, not measured benchmark results. Live OpenRouter metadata covers text-input, text-output models in its public catalog and two curated local choices; it does not cover every provider, model type or local model. Newly discovered entries are unscored, and top-tier status is not independently established. Fit is a relative point score, not a percentage: task suitability is weighted most, with smaller bonuses for practicality and context; your selected priority adds another bonus. Test representative Python tasks, tool calls, latency, failure handling and data rules before production.
